As a user of this app, I want to be able to optimize the amount of money I can make from the lemonade stand and feel accomplished when I am done.

I want to be able to manage my resources to make most money possible.

I want to adapt to given environmental changes and change my strategy day-by-day.

I want to be able to compete against other players.

I want to feel like I can run out of resources and fail.
